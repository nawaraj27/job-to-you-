How it works

Four steps, and you own all of them.

  1. 01

    Post the role with its real requirements

    The skills you list are the skills matching uses. A vague posting produces vague matches, and the form says so while you write it.

  2. 02

    Candidates see why they fit, or why they don't

    People who are clearly short of the requirements are told before they apply, which is better for them and cuts the volume you read.

  3. 03

    Review a ranked pipeline

    Matched skills, missing skills, years of experience and the CV itself, in one screen. Move people between stages and keep notes against them.

  4. 04

    Get verified

    Registration documents and a domain-matched email address. Verified listings carry a badge; unverified ones say that plainly rather than leaving candidates to guess.

What we won't do

Rank candidates for you
Matching compares stated skills against stated requirements and shows its working. It does not score people, and nothing is auto-rejected.
Sell you candidate data
You see a candidate's profile when they apply to your role. Not before, and not for any other role.
Charge per posting
Pricing is per company, so how much you hire never changes what you pay. See pricing.
