{{ site.name }} exists to close that feedback gap. Upload the CV you already send out, and see it the way an applicant tracking system does: what parsed, what didn't, which requirements you match, and which sentences are costing you interviews.

What we believe

  • Feedback should be specific. "Improve your summary" is not feedback; "these six bullets have no measurable result" is.
  • Your words stay yours. Suggested rewrites are shown side by side, with a reason, and you accept or reject each one.
  • Career histories are not uniform. Internships, freelancing, research, volunteering, and career breaks are all real experience.
  • Data belongs to the person it describes. You can export it or delete it whenever you want.

Where we're going

The platform is being built module by module: CV analysis first, then the builder, job matching, interviews, and the employer side. Each one ships when it works properly rather than as a preview.
