{% comment %} The guided flow's step list, and its progress counter. A partial rather than markup inside `guided.html`, because both of the controls in it change it. The step buttons swap the editor region, and this list sits outside that swap — so setting a step aside moved the counter on the server and left the old number on screen, which reads as the button not having worked. Rendered out of band (`oob`) after either command, the same way the assistant's transcript is. Completion is derived from the draft on every render, so a step the candidate has emptied goes back to incomplete instead of staying ticked. An optional step can be set aside, and the counter then stops counting it. That is the difference between a guide and a form: a student, a graduate or a school leaver could not reach the end of a flow whose third question is "Where you've worked" without inventing a job, and the counter sat at "4 of 5" until they did. {% endcomment %} {% url 'resume:builder_act' document_id=document.public_id as act %}
