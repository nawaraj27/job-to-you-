{% comment %} The part of the builder that actually builds the CV. Every section is editable where it sits: rename it, reorder it, hide it, delete it, and type into it. Before this the panel offered move-up, move-down and hide — and nothing at all for the words, so a "CV builder" had no way to enter a job title, a bullet or a summary. Everything below already existed as a command on `BuilderCommandView`; none of it had a control. Every field posts on `change` rather than on a save button. A CV builder with a save button is a CV builder that loses work, and the header already reports what is saved and offers undo. Drag to reorder is layered on top of the arrows rather than replacing them: keyboard and screen-reader users get the buttons, everyone gets the drag, and both post the same commands. {% endcomment %} {% url 'resume:builder_act' document_id=document.public_id as act %}
