Answer aloud

What is recorded, and what happens to it
  • Your microphone is recorded only between "Start answering" and "Stop".
  • The recording is sent to our server, turned into text, and deleted straight away. It is never stored.
  • The camera, if you turn it on, is a preview for you. Video is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • We keep the text of your answer and a few timing numbers — how long you spoke, your pace, long pauses and filler words — with this practice interview. They are deleted with it.
  • Your face, expression, eye contact and appearance are not analysed. Delivery numbers are feedback for you and are never part of your score.

No microphone could be used.

Either no microphone is connected, another app is using it, or this browser cannot record audio. Recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari can. You can type your answer below.

Speech recognition is not set up on this server, so answers cannot be transcribed. You can keep the camera on to rehearse and type your answer below.
