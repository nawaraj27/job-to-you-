Claim before deciding, so two reviewers never work the same submission — and the claim expires, because a reviewer who closes the tab must not remove an item from the queue permanently. The service refuses a decision from anyone who does not hold the live claim; the page only stops offering it. A reason is required to ask for more information or to reject: the company has to know what to do next, and "rejected" with no sentence attached is a support ticket the platform wrote for itself. Documents open through a staff route that writes the file's access log and an audit row before serving them. Decision D-04: a reviewer who is a member of the company, holds a pending invitation to it, or sent the submission is shown why and is not offered the claim; another reviewer takes it.
