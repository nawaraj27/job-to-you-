{% comment %} The confirmation dialog, once, for the whole product. Every `hx-confirm` in the app is routed here by static/js/app.js rather than raising the browser's own `confirm()`. Two mechanisms for the same decision was the problem: a native dialog opens in the operating system's chrome, in the operating system's type, and reads as though a different program asked. Nothing about the call sites changed — they still declare `hx-confirm` and its sentence. The element that triggered the request may add: data-confirm-title heading, default "Are you sure?" data-confirm-label the confirming button, default "Continue" data-confirm-tone "danger" for anything destructive One overlay does the dimming *and* receives the click that dismisses it. Two elements — a tinted layer with a transparent flex layer above it — meant the click landed on the layer that had no handler, and clicking outside did nothing. Inert until opened, so it costs nothing on pages that never confirm. {% endcomment %}
